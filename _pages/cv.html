Biography

I am the Chief Research Officer and a founding member of Cardinal AI Lab, InfRec, leading a research team focused on advancing frontier AI research based on real-world long-horizon trajectory data. My research focuses on recursive self-improvement and long-horizon world interaction, enabling AI agents to accumulate long-term knowledge and autonomously control embodied robots.

I am also pursuing a B.E. in the School of Telecommunication Engineering at Xidian University, advised by Prof. Xiumei Wang. Previously, I was a research intern at Alibaba and TeleAI, and a Research Assistant at the HPC-AI Lab, National University of Singapore.
